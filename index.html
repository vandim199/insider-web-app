A companion for your Insider-style party round. Someone always knows more than they let on.

How a round works
  1. Pick a Master and secretly pick one player to be the Insider. Everyone else is a Detective.
  2. Pass the phone: the Master and the Insider each privately learn which of the five words is correct.
  3. Detectives ask yes/no questions to find the secret word before time runs out.
  4. The Insider already knows it, but has to blend in without getting caught steering the group.
