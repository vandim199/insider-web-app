SECRET WORD

A companion for your Insider-style party round. Someone always knows more than they let on.

How a round works
  1. Distribute roles to players — the Insider may or may not be among you.
  2. The Master and Insider learn the secret word; Detectives do not.
  3. Detectives ask yes/no questions to find the secret word before time runs out.
  4. The Insider knows the word and must secretly steer the team without getting caught.
